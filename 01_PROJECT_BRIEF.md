# GEATZ PORTFOLIO — PROJECT BRIEF

## Project
A premium personal portfolio website for Geatz, positioned as a Video Editor & Content Creator.

## Core Positioning
> I turn ideas into content worth watching.

Supporting philosophy:
> Content first. Effects second.

## Primary Capabilities
- Video Editing
- Content Creation
- Script Writing
- Short-form Content
- Storytelling
- Content Thinking / Strategy
- AI-assisted creative workflows

## Audience
- Potential clients
- Recruiters
- Creators
- Small brands
- People looking for content/video editing support

## Brand Personality
Minimal, cinematic, editorial, creative, confident, human, intentional.

## Important Positioning Rule
Do not present Geatz as:
- A generic beginner editor
- A professional motion-graphics-only designer
- A cinematic filmmaker
- Someone who “does everything”

The website should communicate:
> Geatz understands content, not just editing software.

## Main Site Pages
1. Home
2. Work
3. Create
4. Think
5. About
6. Contact

## Contact
Only:
- Email
- Instagram
- YouTube

Do NOT add LinkedIn or Resume.

## Language
Website default: English.

About page includes:
> English ↔ Tanglish

The switch should adapt the copy naturally rather than performing literal word-for-word translation.

## Technical Priorities
1. Content
2. Responsiveness
3. Performance
4. Interaction
5. Visual polish

The website must work beautifully on mobile, tablet, laptop, desktop and large screens.
